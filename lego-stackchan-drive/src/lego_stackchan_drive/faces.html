LEGO drive mode

Use the selected ACT model. Loading opens the driving window and sets the camera to that model’s training angle. Put the robot on its training track and center the controller sticks.

Reading driving mode…

Hold Space in the driving window or R1 to let the model drive (up to 20 seconds). Release to stop, or use arrows/sticks to take over. Press R to start/stop recording inference video and decisions; these clips are excluded from training. T/G adjust tilt. Face and speech controls stay available. Stop returns the head to manual posing.
